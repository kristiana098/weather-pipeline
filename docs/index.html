Weather Poem

**English Version:**

Weather's gentle hand weaves a tale,
In three cities, diverse and frail.

Aalborg's misty veil descends to earth,
A cool 10.9°C, with rain's gentle birth.
A gentle breeze stirs at 14 km/h pace,
A soothing calm, in a quiet space.

Athens' warmth beckons, a radiant smile,
27.3°C shines bright, with a gentle guile.
No rain falls, just a light caress,
A Mediterranean dream, to caress.

Copenhagen's chill, in a drizzly haze,
10.0°C whispers secrets, in a rainy daze.
A windy gust, at 17 km/h's wild sway,
A city's melancholy, on a gray day.

**Der er ikke kun én vej**  **(There's not just one way)**

Men hvis du ville være her i morgen,
Så ville jeg sige, at Athen skulle være formoden.

Men hvis du elsker koldt, så Aalborg er ikke dårligt at være i.
Og hvis du er sulten efter regn og vind, så Copenhagen er ikke et dårligt spøgelse.
