Weather Poem

**English:**

Aalborg's warmth, a gentle breeze,
Copenhagen's calm, in parallel seas.
Athens' fire, a radiant face,
Which city's charm will take its place?

The data speaks, a statistical test,
Aalborg's heat, without a guest.
Copenhagen's balance, serene and still,
Athens' heat, with a hint of chill.

Tomorrow's forecast, a traveler's guide,
Aalborg's warmth, side by side.
Copenhagen's peace, a place to rest,
Or perhaps, Athens' heat, is truly the best?

**Danmarks vinter kan være en kølig affære.**

Men hvis vi sammenligner de døgnmålinger:

Aalborg's varme, en blid brise,
Københavns skønhed, i parallelle havlige dale.
Athen's flamme, et radierende ansigt,
Hvor byen har karakteren, der vil være bedst i morgen?

Dataet taler, et statistisk test,
Aalborgs varme, uden gæst.
Københavns balance, fredsom og stille,
Athen's varme, med en skygge af kulde.

I morgens vejret, en rejselfører,
Aalborg's varme, langs side.
Københavns fred, et sted at hvile,
Eller måske, Athen's varme, er virkelig det bedste?

In Danish: If we're comparing Aalborg and Copenhagen, then Athens looks quite hot and may be the nicest city to go to.
