Weather Poem

**English:**

The weather whispers tales of three cities fair,
Each with its own charm, beyond compare.

Aalborg's rain whispers secrets in the night,
As wind and water dance, a melancholy sight.
Copenhagen's drizzle kisses the morning dew,
A gentle lover's caress, both soothing and new.

Athens, oh Athens, shines like a radiant sun,
Warming the heart, and having fun.
The temperature rises, a balmy delight,
A perfect day to stroll, beneath the warm light.

So I would choose to be in Athens today,
Where the sun shines bright, and the heart feels at play.
Tomorrow, I'll leave the rain behind,
In Athens, where joy and beauty entwine.

**Danmark ( Danish ):**

Veget siger fort fortællinger om tre byer skønne,
Hver med sin egen charme, uden sammenligning.

Aalborgs regn hvisker hemmeligheder i natten,
Så vind og vand danser, et melankolsk syn.
Københavnses dråbe kysser morgenskyet,
En blide elskerens omfavning, både syngende og ny.

Athens, oh, det er som en lysende sol,
Fører hjertet og giver glæde.
Temperaturen stiger, en varm og behagelig fornemmelse,
En perfekt dag for at gå uden skyggen af lys.

Så ville jeg vælge at være i Athen om aften,
Hvor solen skiner lys, og hjertet føler sig hjemme.
I morgen, vil jeg läge regnen bag mig,
I Athen, hvor lykke og skønhed forgrenas.

(Note: As I don't have the specific weather information of tomorrow, I made a comparison of the cities given and a hypothetical choice of where it would be "nicest" based on provided data.)
