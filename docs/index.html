Weather Poem

**English Version:**

Across the seas, a tale is told
Of three cities, weather to behold
Aalborg's grey sky weeps with rain
16.1°C, a gentle summer refrain

Athens's sun beats down so bright
32.8°C, a scorching summer's night
Copenhagen's charm, amidst the rain
18.1°C, a Danish summer's gentle gain

But where would I choose to roam tomorrow?
Perhaps Copenhagen, where the rain brings a gentle glow
The wind whispers secrets, as the droplets play
A symphony of rain, to drive the blues away

**Dansk Version:**

Over havene fortælles en historie
Om tre byer, vejret at beundre
Aalborgs sky er grå ogregnende
16,1°C, en blid sommerens tale

Athens solstråler ned med stor kraft
32,8°C, en brændende sommernats kærlighed
Københavns charme, i den regnfulde dag
18,1°C, en dansk sommers mild og ro

Men hvor ville jeg vælge at rejse i morgen?
Måske København, hvor regnen bringer en mild glød
Vinden hvisler hemmeligheder, mens dråberne spiller
En regnfuld symfoni, der sender blåa væk

Note: Please keep in mind that weather forecasts are subject to change, and these comparisons are based on the given data. If you're planning a trip, it's always best to check the current weather forecast before making any decisions.
