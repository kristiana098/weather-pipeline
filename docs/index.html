**In Perfect Harmony**

Three cities, each with its own beat,
Aalborg's warmth, a gentle treat,
22.5°C, a summer's delight,
0.0mm rain, a cloudless sight.

Athens, the cradle of old,
Echoes of gods, stories untold,
24.1°C, with a hint of dew,
1.4mm rain, a gentle break-through.

Copenhagen, the crown of north,
Where Nordic charm, meets urban birth,
23.1°C, a pleasant pace,
0.0mm rain, a smile on every face.

But where is it nicest to be tomorrow?
Let the winds whisper, the sun shine bright,
I'd say, with a heart full of cheer,
Aalborg's the place, to banish all fear.

For in Aalborg, the weather's a dream,
22.5°C, a perfect summer theme,
So if you're looking for a place to roam,
Aalborg's the city, where the heart finds home.

**Hvor er det dejligst at være i morgen?** 
Lad vindene hvisle, solen skinne klar,
og det er på Aalborg, du skal være varm.

For i Aalborg er vejhætterne en drøm,
22,5°C, en perfekt sommerstemning,
så hvis du søger et sted at lette på,
er Aalborg byen, hvor hjertet finder hjem.
