Weather Poem

English:

Aalborg's summer breeze whispers low,
A gentle zephyr, soft and slow,
Athen's heat beats down, a fiery sway,
Copenhagen's storms, come what may.

Amongst this trio, which one to choose?
Aalborg's calm, or Athens's heat's dark muse?
Copenhagen's rain, a melancholy grey,
Or perhaps none, and wait for a brighter day?

The winds may howl, in Aalborg's west,
The sun may beat, in Athens's fiery crest,
Copenhagen's storms, may bring a lonely sigh,
But where is nicest to be, and why?

Tomorrow's forecast, shows a radiant sky,
Athens shines, with a warm, and sunny guise,
So pack your bags, and head to the land,
Of ancient ruins, and golden sands, in hand.

Dansk:

Aalborgs sommerbreeze hvisler blidt,
En blid vind, der svinger let og mildt,
Athens' varme banker op, en ildsvangrende gang,
Copenhagen's tordenregn,  kommer hvad som helst.

I denne trio, hvilken af byerne skal jeg vælge?
Aalborgs stilhed eller Athen's varme sorte vinge?
Copenhagens regn,  et melankolsk gråt
Eller måske ingen, og vente på en lysegrønn dag?

Vinden må hvæse, i Aalborgs vest,
Solen må bank, i Athen's ildridste,
Copenhagens regn, må bringe en ensomt sind,
Men hvor er det bedst at være, og hvorfor?

I morgenens vejrudsigt, viser en lysende himmel,
Athen glår med en varm og solskinslige gestus,
Så pak dine tæpper og tag afsted i landet,
Af gamle ruiner og guldene sand, i hånd.
