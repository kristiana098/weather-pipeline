Weather Poem

**English**

Aalborg, Athens, and Copenhagen, three cities so different in sight,
Weather forecasts reveal a varying fate, a tale of contrasts to ignite.

Aalborg's clouded sky weeps with gentle rain,
While Athens shines bright, a warm and golden gain.
Copenhagen's breeze whispers secrets, strong and fast,
A summer's warmth that lingers, leaving the past.

But where would we rather be tomorrow's delight?
Perhaps in Athens, where sunshine shines without a fight.
No rain to mar the scene, a day of bliss to share,
In this city of ancient charm, where warmth and joy are rare.

**Dansk**

Aalborg, Athen og København, tre byer så forskellige med øje,
Vejrforudsigelser afslører en varierende skæbne, et eventyr af kontraster til at tænde.

Aalborgs skyede himmel græder med blide regntårer,
Samtidig lyser Athen op, et koldt og guldagtigt vinder.
Københavns bris hvisler hemmeligheder, kraftig og rasende,
Et sommers varme som lader sig glede, forladende fortiden.

Men hvor ville vi hellere være imorges fornøjelse?
Måske i Athen, hvor solen skinner uden kamp.
Ingen regn til at ødelægge den udtrykte scene,
I denne by af antikt charme, hvor varme og glæde er sjældent.

In the comparison, Aalborg appears to have the most unpleasant weather, given the rain. Between Athens and Copenhagen, Athens has the nicest forecast due to its dry and sunny conditions, making it the preferred destination for the following day.
