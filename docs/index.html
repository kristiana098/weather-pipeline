Weather Poem

**English:**

Weather's gentle dance, a tale of three,
Aalborg whispers secrets, soft and low.
Cool misty mornings, 10°C's gentle breeze,
3 millimeters of rain, a soothing ease.

But in the east, where sunshine meets the sky,
Athens radiates warmth, a Mediterranean sigh.
25°C's caress, dew kisses away,
A gentle breeze, 17.3 km/h, to sway.

Copenhagen's gentle calm, a Nordic peace,
14°C's serenity, the wind whispers release.
17.6 km/h's gentle caress, rain's sweet lullaby,
A tranquil morning, Copenhagen's gentle sigh.

Tomorrow, perhaps, in Athens' radiant glow,
Warmth and sunbeams welcome, a traveler's delight to know.
Leave Aalborg's misty shroud, and Copenhagen's chill,
Find solace in Athens' warmth, and let your spirit fulfill.

**Dansketranslation:**

Vejrforløbet, en historie af tre,
Aalborg hvisler hemmeligheder, blidt og lavt.
Kulde morgener, 10°C blid vandring,
3 millimeter regn, en sær skønhed.

Men i øst, hvor solen møder himlen,
Athen sender varme, en mediterransk sukk.

25°C kys, tåge borte skjuler,
 En blid vind, 17.3 km/t, der søger.
Copenhagen's blide fred, en nordisk ro,
14°C’s rolige fred, vinden hvisler løse.

17,6 km/t’s blide vise, regn’s lullaby,
En rolig morgen, Copenhagens blide sukk.

I morgen måske, i Athen's varme skær,
Sol og varme varsel, en rejsendes glemme.
Lad Aalborg’s tåge skjule, og Copenhagens kulde,
Find ro i Athen's varme, og lad dine ånd gennemføre.
