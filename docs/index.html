Weather Poem

**English Version**

A whirlwind of contrasts, they dance and play,
Three cities, each with its own weather sway.

Aalborg, a temperate soul, with gentle breeze,
18 degrees, a calm, serene ease.

Athens, a scorching heart, with fiery soul,
33 degrees, a sweltering, burning role.

Copenhagen, a dramatic tale, with rain and gale,
17 degrees, a tempestuous, wild hail.

Tomorrow, if I had to choose a place,
I'd flee to Athens, and its warm sunny face.
For in its heat, I'd find a peaceful nest,
And bask in the joy, of its Mediterranean rest.

**Dansk version**

En storme vej af sammenligninger, de danser og spiller,
Tre byer, hver med sin egen vejrsag.

Aalborg, en tempereret sjæl, med mild bise,
18 grader, en rolig, stillehed.

Athens, et stråleende hjerte, med en varm og frisk sjæl,
33 grader, en svulmende og brændende rolle.

København, en dramatisk fortælling, med regn og storm,
17 grader, en turbulent og vild vindstorm.

I morgen, hvis jeg skulle vælge et sted,
Jeg ville flugte til Athen og dens varme sol.
For i dens varme ville jeg finde et roligt red,
Og bade i lykken, af dens middelhavsværd.

Note: Please note that this poem is a subjective comparison and not a scientifically-based weather analysis. For any actual weather forecast, please consult a reliable weather source.
