Weather Poem

**Poetic Comparison: "Skiftende Vera" (Changing Weather)**

Som dagens skyaften møder (As day meets evening sky)
To byer strækker sig: Aalborg, København og Athen. 
Der ligger en spænding i luften, en uendelig måde at blive inspireret af skiftende vera (weather).

Aalborg med sine 14.9 grader og vindens hvisken (whispering wind), 
en smule kølig, men alligevel velkommen i sin målløse glæde.

København, med sit 16.2 grader og stormens fare (storm's fear), 
en by i flugt fra koldt blåt vand (cold blue water).

Men da vi ser Athen, med sit 24.8 grader og det himmelske lys (heavenly light), 
kunne det være, at denne er byen, hvor solen kæmper til sejr (solar victory).

Men hvor skulle den klokkestemme (clock's voice) bede om at være i morgen?

Det ville være i Athen, da morgensolen strækker sine udstrakte arme (sun's extended arms), 
og byen, der stræber hen til det blå (blue sky) giver os nye muligheder.

**Translated to English:**

As day meets the evening sky, three cities stand tall: Aalborg, Copenhagen, and Athens. 
The air is filled with suspense, an infinite way to get inspired by changing weather.

Aalborg with its 14.9 degrees and the whispering wind,
a little cool, yet still welcome in its futile joy.

Copenhagen, with its 16.2 degrees and the storm's fear,
a city in flight from the cold blue water.

But when we see Athens, with its 24.8 degrees and the heavenly light,
it could be that this is the city where the sun fights for victory.

But where would the clock's voice ask to be in the morning?

It would be in Athens, as morning sun stretches out its arms,
and the city, striving towards the blue, gives us new chances.

So, det er Athen, som vinder dagen, (Athens, the winner of the day)
