Weather Poem

**In Harmonious Contrast**

Like three gentle sisters, the cities dance in the wind,
Each with its unique voice, a melody to unwind.

Aalborg whispers soft caresses, a gentle 22's sway,
A haven from the heat, where summer's warmth fades away.
The city's breeze, a soothing lullaby, a peaceful nest,

Athens sings a fiery aria, 35.7 degrees ablaze,
A fiery passion that ignites, with a fervent, burning gaze.
The city's heart, a burning flame, where love and beauty reign,

Copenhagen's gentle melody, a 25.2 degree refrain,
A soothing balance, where warmth and comfort intertwine in perfect harmony's strain.

So, where should you be tomorrow, amidst this waltz of weather's sway?
Perhaps Aalborg, where summer's gentle breeze will whisper "Stay",
Or will it be Copenhagen's gentle dance, that beckons you to play?
The choice is yours, but Athens' fiery heartbeat will be waiting each day.

**Og hvad hvis vi skal vælge, hvilken by til morgendagen?**
Kan vi ikke vælge det, hvor sommeren bliver en hyggelig ven?
Måske Aalborg, hvor en blærer som en sœgende sang, siger "bliv"?
Eller måske Københavns mildere dans, der inviterer dig til at spille?
Valget er ditt, men Athen's elskede hjerter vil vente hver dag.

Note: The Danish sentences offer an alternative perspective on the cities and the decision of which city to choose.
