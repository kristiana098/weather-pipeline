Weather Poem

**English Version**

Softly weeps the rain in Aalborg's shade,
A gentle mist to soothe the summer's heat displayed,
But nearby lies Athens, bathed in golden light,
A warmth that beckons all, on this balmy night.

Copenhagen's winds, a gentle, whispering breeze,
Carry scents of blooming flowers, in perfumed ease.
Yet, which city shall we call our haven today,
And smile at tomorrow's forecast, come what may?

Tomorrow's charm, lies in Aalborg's misty veil,
Where the rain's caress, shall bring a soothing tale,
So pack your bags, and head to Aalborg's shore,
Where the rain's sweet song, shall lull you evermore.

**Dansk Version**

Sødt viger regnen i Aalborgs skygge,
En mild tåge til at trøste sommersolens varme,
Men ikke langt fra står Athen, badet i guldigt lys,
Et varme, der kalder på alle, på denne varme nat.

Københavnens vind, er en blid, hvislende brise,
Der bærer dobbelt- og blomstens duft i parfumeret let,
Men hvilken by skal vi kalde vår hørende i dag,
Og smil til morgendagens forudsigelse, hvad enten det skylder eller giver?

Morgendagens charme, finder man i Aalborgs tåge,
Hvor regnens berøring, skal bringe en trøstelig fortælling,
Så pakk bagene og følg med til Aalborgs strand,
Hvor regnens søngende sang, skal døf dig uden ende.

Note: This poetic comparison aims to capture the unique characteristics of each city's weather and invites readers to imagine themselves in each place. While Aalborg's rain and Copenhagen's breeze are pleasant, it concludes that Athens is likely too hot, leaving Aalborg as the most appealing choice for a gentle, calming atmosphere.
