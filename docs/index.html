Weather Poem

**A Poetic Comparison**

The sun beats down on three cities fair,
Aalborg, Athens, and Copenhagen, each with its own care.

Aalborg's warmth is gentle, a soothing breeze so light,
25.5 degrees, a perfect day to delight.
The wind whispers secrets, a gentle 18.7 km/h pace,
A peaceful haven, where one can find their space.

Athens, a city of ancient charm and might,
Reaches 33.3 degrees, a scorching heat in sight.
The sun beats down relentlessly, a force so bold,
One might seek shade, to avoid feeling cold.

Copenhagen, a city of beauty and design,
Reaches 28.2 degrees, a warm and pleasant shine.
The wind stirs gently, a 15.8 km/h caress,
A city that beckons, to come and be possessed.

So where to go tomorrow, with these words in mind?
Copenhagen's gentle warmth, would be left behind.
Athens, though hot, would offer a vibrant thrill,
But Aalborg's peaceful charm, would be the most appealing still.

**Og hvem ville være i Aalborg i morgen?**

Med disse ord i sind, ville en selv måtte spørge:
Hvor ville være i morgen?
Oplysningerne siger, at der vil være klokken 25,5 grader i Aalborg.
En kæmpe fornøjelse at opholde sig der.

Copenhagen, selvom det er varmt, vil dog være en skøn oplevelse,
Men Aalborgs stilfulde ro, vil være det bedste valg at vælge.

Men så spørger man sig selv: hvordan er det i Athen i morgen?
 Der er selvom det er vildt varmt, men en god udfordring til at være der.
