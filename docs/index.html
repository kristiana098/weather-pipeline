Weather Poem

**English Version**

In Aalborg's summer breeze so fine,
22.3 degrees, a gentle wind that shines.
But then to Athens, a scorching heat,
36.1 degrees, our souls can't compete.

Copenhagen stands, a moderate delight,
22.8 degrees, a pleasant morning light.
While Aalborg's winds whisper secrets low,
And Athens burns, with temperatures aglow.

Tomorrow's forecast shines so bright,
Aalborg's charm will warm our night.
Soft winds caress, a perfect guest,
In Aalborg, all can take a rest.

**Danske Version**

I Aalborgs sommerbregning så fin,
22,3 grader, en blid vind der skinner.
Men så til Athen, varmen så hærget,
36,1 grader, sjælene kan ikke konkurrere med.

København står, en modig sommerdag,
22,8 grader, en smuk morgenstråle.
 Mens Aalborgs winds hvisker hemmeligheder lavt,
 Og Athen brænder, med temperaturer i glød.

I morgen af forveksler lys så klart,
Aalborgs charme vil varme vores nat.
Blide vindstød omfavner, en perfekt gæst.
I Aalborg, kan alle få hvilest.

Note: This is a short poetic comparison where the cities are compared based on their weather conditions. The poem concludes that Aalborg might be the most pleasant place to be tomorrow, given its weather.
