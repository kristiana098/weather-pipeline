Weather Poem

**English**

Aalborg – 5.6 °C, wind 11.2 km/h, no rain,  
a quiet, frosted hush that sighs in steel.  

Athens – 21.4 °C, wind 18.8 km/h, no rain,  
sun‑kissed streets that glow, a warm caress.  

Copenhagen – 6.9 °C, wind 16.9 km/h, no rain,  
a brisk, steady note in a chilled air.  

*Tomorrow is best in Athens, where the heat sings softly.*

---

**Dansk**

Aalborg – 5,6 °C, vind 11,2 km/t, ingen regn,  
en stille, frostet stillhed der ånder i stål.  

Athen – 21,4 °C, vind 18,8 km/t, ingen regn,  
solkysset gader, som gløder, varm kærtegn.  

København – 6,9 °C, vind 16,9 km/t, ingen regn,  
et koldt, fastt notat i en kølig luft.  

*I morgen er det bedst i Athen, hvor varmen synger blidt.*
