Weather Poem

**In Harmonious Contrast**

Aalborg's warmth is like a summer's sigh,
18.6 degrees, a gentle, whispering sky.
A hint of rain, a soft caress, it brings,
A wind that whispers secrets, as gentle things.

Athens, ablaze, in radiant light,
29.7 degrees, a fiery, passionate night.
No rain to soothe, no breeze to sway,
Just heat that beckons, come what may.

Copenhagen's calm, a peaceful nest,
20.3 degrees, a gentle, summer rest.
Silent skies, a quiet, still air,
A city slumber, without a single care.

**Tomorrow's Delight**

Hvor er det lækkert at være i morgen? (Where is it lovely to be tomorrow?)

Aalborg or Copenhagen, take your pick,
Both offer warmth and calm, a gentle trick.
But if I must choose, I'd say Copenhagen's charm,
A gentle evening, a peaceful, summer's form.

Copenhagen bød på 16°C, 0,0 mm regn. (Copenhagen offered 16°C, 0.0 mm rain)

So pack your bags, and head to Denmark's shore,
Copenhagen awaits, with a gentle, summer's score.
