Weather Poem

**In a Whisper of Wind and Rain**

Two cities meet in harmony,
Aalborg and Copenhagen, side by side in symphony,
Their weather whispers secrets, of a summer's gentle breeze,
Where the temperature sways, and raindrops lightly freeze.

Aalborg's warmth, a cozy delight,
20.8 degrees, on a summer's night,
A gentle shower, a sprinkle of dew,
A wind that whispers, "Come, and dance with me, anew."

But then, a rival, a sun-kissed throne,
Athens beckons, where the gods once roamed,
29.9 degrees, a fiery passion play,
A wind that stirs, a gentle summer day.

**Hvor er det bedst at være i dag?**

Hvis jeg kunne vælge, hvor det var bedst at være i dag...
Jeg ville vælge Aalborg, med sin behagelige temperatur,
Og den smukke, nærmest skyfri himmel.

**But where is it nicest to be tomorrow?**

If I could choose, where it's nicest to be tomorrow...
I would choose Aalborg, with its pleasant temperature,
And the lovely, almost cloudless sky.

Note: Please keep in mind that the weather forecast mentioned in the prompt is from a past date, the answer for where it is nicest to be tomorrow may be different.
