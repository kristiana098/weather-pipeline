Weather Poem

**English**

Like three sisters, each with her own face,
The weather weaves a different pace.

Aalborg's gentle breeze carries the day,
A mild 12°C, with nary a drop to say.
But Athens, the warmth-seeker, spreads her gold,
A radiant 26.9°C, young and bold.

But Copenhagen, the Nordic heart, does mourn,
A drizzly 11.1°C, with a gentle scorn.
Rain falls soft, yet whispers a tale,
Of autumn's melancholy, and winter's veil.

If you ask where it's nice to be tomorrow,
Perhaps Athens' sun will guide you, come what may.
Or if you prefer a milder air,
Aalborg's gentle breeze may be your heart's care.
