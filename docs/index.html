Weather Poem

In summer's golden light, the cities sway,
Each with its own charm, on this day's display.

Aalborg, where the wind whispers low,
A gentle breeze, amidst the warm glow.
Twenty-two degrees, a pleasant nest,
Where the North Sea's calmness finds its rest.

Athens, the cradle of ancient might,
Sizzling at thirty, a fiery delight.
Heat that's unrelenting, a challenge to bear,
Thirty degrees, a test of patience rare.

Copenhagen, the Nordic charm so free,
A balmy twenty-two, a haven for you and me.
A peaceful morning, with the wind at rest,
Twenty-five degrees, a promise of the day's best.

Tomorrow, if I had to choose,
I'd sail to Copenhagen, with its gentle breeze, a calm reprieve.
The wind's whispers sweet, a soothing melody,
A place where summer's charm, is music to be.
