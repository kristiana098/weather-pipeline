Weather Poem

**Poetic Comparison of the Weather**

**Danish Version:**
En vejviser gennem været dogn for dogn,
Tres byer, hvor hver sin vej til vej.
Aalborgs regn, en fugleven som søger nogle steder mod hjem.
Athens varme, en dronning, der hersker over en by.
Copenhagen mildt, en kvinde, der giver hånd på vandring.

Men hvor er det mest dejligt at være i morgen?
Måske i Aalborg med mosevand til venstre.
Men hvor er det, som føles bedst?
Måske kun i Athen, hvor hver solskinsstrå, en beretning åbner.

**English Version:**
A guide through the weather from day to day,
Three cities, each with its own path to the way.
Aalborg's rain, a bird's flight seeking shelter away.
Athens' heat, a queen ruling over a city.
Copenhagen's mildness, a guiding hand on wandering.

But where is it most delightful to be tomorrow?
Perhaps in Aalborg with marshland to the left.
But where is it, as it feels best?
Perhaps only in Athens, where each sunbeam opens a story.

Note: The descriptions are poetic and not meant to be taken literally. The comparisons are subjective and intended to evoke a sense of atmosphere and feeling.
