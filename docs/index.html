Weather Poem

**English**

Aalborg shivers at two‑point‑five, wind’s a quiet drum,  
Copenhagen huffs at four‑point‑three, a gale that hums,  
Athens sways at nineteen, a gentle rain’s caress—  
Tomorrow the sun leans on Athens, the softest address.  

**Danish**

Aalborg kvidrer ved to‑point‑fem, vindens tystes tromme,  
København hufter ved fire‑point‑tre, en vinde som trommer,  
Athen vugger ved nitten, en mild regens kærtegn—  
I morgen lyser solen i Athen, den blødeste scene.
