Weather Poem

**In English**

Soft whispers of the morning breeze,
Three cities awake, each with its pleas.
Aalborg's warmth beckons, gentle and bright,
17.9 degrees, a perfect night.

Athens, a gem, with sunshine in its eyes,
21.1 degrees, a warmth that never dies.
But Aalborg's charm, a quiet delight,
Lures in the hearts, with a gentle light.

Copenhagen's chill, a crisp air to share,
16.3 degrees, a freshness beyond compare.
Yet Aalborg's beauty, a haven to stay,
A gentle haven, from the world's mad sway.

Tomorrow's forecast, a tale to tell,
Aalborg's winds, at 17.3 km/h, a gentle spell.
The warmest city, with sunshine in its soul,
Aalborg's the choice, where hearts make their goal.

**I en dansk version**

Morgensolens hviskende lyd,
Tre byer vakler, hver med sit bud.
Aalborgs varme udsender, blide og lys,
17,9 grader, en perfekt nat.

Athens et smykke, med sollys i øjnene,
21,1 grader, en varme der aldrig tager opløs.
Men Aalborg smukhed, en stille charme,
Trækker i hjerterne, med en blid lys.

Københavns kulde, en frisk luft til deling,
16,3 grader, en friskhed uden sidestykke.
Men Aalborg skønhed, en hvid have til stede,
En stille oase, ude fra verdens galende væb.

I morgendagens vejr, en fortælling at fortælle,
Aalborgs vind, på 17,3 kilometer i timen, en blid spid til tale.
Den varmeste by, med sollys i sin sjæl,
Aalborg er valget, hvor hjerter finder deres formål.

Note: Please note that the translations have a poetic tone, but they are not direct word-for-word translations for the purpose of poetic style.
