Weather Poem

**A poetic comparison of the weather**

In the realm of climate, three cities stand,
Aalborg, Athens, and Copenhagen, hand in hand.
Their weather whispers secrets, of warmth and of breeze,
Each one a tale of its own, in an ever-changing ease.

Aalborg wakes with a gentle morning light,
18 degrees and clear skies, a pleasant sight.
The wind whispers secrets, at 25.9 kilometers fast,
A refreshing start to the day, that will surely last.

Athens basks in the sun's warm, golden rays,
29.8 degrees of heat, on a Mediterranean day.
The breeze stirs lazily, at 13.4 kilometers slow,
A gentle caress, that only the gods would know.

Copenhagen's charm, lies in its crisp morning air,
21.4 degrees and rain-free, a Scandinavian flair.
The wind whispers secrets, at 22.7 kilometers wide,
A fresh and invigorating start, that will take you for a ride.

But if I had to choose, where would I be tomorrow?
In Athens, perhaps, where the heat will linger and borrow,
From the gods of old, who once roamed free,
And the breeze will dance, in perfect harmony.

**Og hvis jeg skulle vælge hvor jeg ville være i morgen...**

Måske Athen, hvor solen vil lyse og bane,
 Og brisen vil svæve, som en danserinde med glans.
Hvor varmen vil være, som en middelhavskys,
 Og det gamle græske ånden vil være til stede.

I Athen kan jeg drage ud og opleve,
 Den varme og frugtbare jord, der giver liv og glede.
 Og den kolde brise, der vil køle og rengøre,
 Min sjæl og min krop, for at være klar og stram til morgen.
